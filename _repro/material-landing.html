Everything you would expect

It's just Markdown

Focus on the content of your documentation and create a professional static site in minutes. No need to know HTML, CSS or JavaScript – let Material for MkDocs do the heavy lifting for you.

Works on all devices

Serve your documentation with confidence – Material for MkDocs automatically adapts to perfectly fit the available screen estate, no matter the type or size of the viewing device.

Made to measure

Make it yours – change the colors, fonts, language, icons, logo, and more with a few lines of configuration. Material for MkDocs can be easily extended and provides many options to alter appearance and behavior.

Fast and lightweight

Don't let your users wait – get incredible value with a small footprint by using one of the smallest self-contained themes available, with excellent performance out of the box.

Maintain ownership

Own your documentation's complete sources and outputs, guaranteeing both integrity and security – no need to entrust the backbone of your product knowledge to third-party platforms.

Open Source

You're in good company – trusted by more than 20,000 individuals and organizations. Licensed under MIT, Material for MkDocs is available and free forever.

More than just a static site

Built-in search

Impress your users with an excellent client-side search implementation – no server needed. Ships with support for more than 60 languages and advanced features like scoring, term highlighting, and lookahead.

Code annotations

Add anything you like next to your code blocks – text, images, code blocks, call-outs, content tabs, even interactive elements. Code annotations are fully responsive and printable.

Social cards

Make an impression when your documentation is shared on social media – generate beautiful preview images automatically for every page, with zero configuration required.

10,000+ icons and emojis

Bring your documentation to life – choose from over 10,000 icons and emojis, and use them anywhere in your Markdown, with support for custom icon sets.
